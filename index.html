Who I Am

A bit about me

I'm a Master's student in Sustainable Finance at BI Norwegian Business School, Oslo — currently writing my thesis on renewable energy and data-driven decision making. I grew up navigating three cultures (Indian, Norwegian, global), which shaped my curiosity and comfort with complexity.

What drives me is the gap between sustainability ambition and sustainability execution. I've seen first-hand at Orkla Foods Norge and the Norwegian Refugee Council how the right data structure — clean, trusted, and understood — can change the decisions people make. I want to be the person who builds that bridge.

Outside of data and finance, I've organized Norway's largest student sustainability conference, cooked and donated meals to charity, babysat children with special needs, and worked as a cashier and chef. I believe in showing up fully — wherever that is.
