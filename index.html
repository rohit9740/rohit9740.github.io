Who I Am

A bit about me

I'm a Master's student in Sustainable Finance at BI Norwegian Business School — writing my thesis on renewable energy and data-driven decision making. Born Indian, raised across three cultures, now firmly rooted in Oslo.

What drives me is the gap between sustainability ambition and sustainability execution. I've seen first-hand at Orkla Foods Norge and the Norwegian Refugee Council how the right data structure — clean, trusted, understood — changes the decisions people make. I want to be the person who builds that bridge.

Outside work: I've organized Norway's largest student sustainability conference, led a charity cooking club, babysat children with special needs, and worked as a chef and cashier for four years. I show up fully — wherever that is.
